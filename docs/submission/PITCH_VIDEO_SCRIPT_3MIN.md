  - Text badge: *"700M+ People Excluded by Text Fact-Checking"*.

In rural and oral-first communities, life-saving information spreads through 30-second WhatsApp voice notes. ### 🎬 Scene 3: The Live Working Demo (The Proof & Socratic Dialogue)
* **Time:** `0:50 – 1:52` (62 seconds)
* **Visual on Screen (Screen recording of live VeriVoice Web Talk Room & Discord/WhatsApp Bot):**
  - **Action 1 (Voice In):** User taps the Acoustic Core and speaks an authentic Urdu health rumor:  
    *“کیا پولیو کے قطرے بچوں کے لیے محفوظ ہیں؟” (Are polio drops safe for children?)*
  - **Action 2 (Real-Time Retrieval & 3D Evidence Canvas):** The screen reveals instant speech transcription, 12-domain routing (`HEALTH`), and the 3D Evidence Constellation converging as official WHO & PAHO records are retrieved.
  - **Action 3 (Spoken Output Playback):** User hears the crisp neural Urdu voice response playing out loud:  
    *“جی ہاں، عالمی ادارہ صحت (WHO) کے مطابق پولیو ویکسین مکمل طور پر محفوظ ہے...”*
  - **Action 4 (Socratic Explanation):** Highlight the Socratic feedback teaching the user *why* the claim is true and *how* to evaluate the primary source.
  - **Action 5 (The Zero-Hallucination Fallback):** Quick split-screen demonstration of an unverified wild rumor: VeriVoice immediately flags `UNCERTAIN` rather than guessing.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Simply speak or forward an audio note. In under two seconds, VeriVoice transcribes the dialect, routes across 12 domain engines, and queries primary institutional databases like WHO, NOAA, and NASA. Instead of just slapping a true or false label, VeriVoice uses Socratic dialogue to explain why a claim is deceptive and teaches critical thinking. And unlike commercial chatbots that hallucinate false confidence, VeriVoice enforces strict zero-hallucination bounds. If primary scientific evidence doesn't exist, it refuses to guess."*
