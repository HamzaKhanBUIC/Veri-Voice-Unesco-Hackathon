## 2. Problem Statement: The Infodemic in an Age of AI

Our team did not pick this problem from a journal article. We grew up around it.

In Karachi, we saw relatives hesitate to vaccinate their children after hearing an alarming forwarded audio message on WhatsApp. In Mexico, we watched family group chats circulate doctored medical cures during seasonal outbreaks. In Indonesia, elders in our neighborhoods trusted anonymous voice memos over local doctors. None of them were going to read a dense, 2,000-word English fact-checking article, but all of them listened to audio messages sent by people they knew. VeriVoice exists to close that specific gap: between what people hear on their phones and what they can actually verify.

Online platforms now prioritize algorithmic engagement over factual accuracy (Reuters Institute, 2026). Young people aged 8 to 17 face the brunt of this shift. While 62% of youth say they feel confident online, most struggle in practice to spot synthetic media, disguised ads, or deepfakes (Ofcom, 2026). This confidence-ability gap grows wider as feed fatigue drives users away from reading and toward 30-second audio and video snippets.

In rural and semi-urban communities across the Global South, text-heavy fact-checking sites leave out more than 700 million people who cannot read well or who speak regional languages (UNESCO, 2021).

At the same time, conversational AI has introduced a new vulnerability. Adolescents often mistake simulated empathy for genuine understanding, leaving them open to unverified medical or scientific claims generated by chatbots (American Psychological Association, 2026). Students also report relying on AI for schoolwork without receiving any instruction on how to evaluate sources or spot hallucinations (Muhammad Rafique et al., 2026).

Flagging claims as "True" or "False" does not build lasting habits. Young people need an interactive, voice-based tool that uses Socratic questions to help them understand how misinformation works.

> **THE HEARTBEAT CRISIS & LITERACY DIVIDE**  
> *“62% of youth feel confident online, yet cannot detect synthetic deception. Text-heavy fact-checking fails 700M+ oral-first individuals. VeriVoice bridges this divide by turning viral audio into Socratic voice conversations grounded in verified science.”*

## 3. Project Objectives

Our primary mission is to give young people the cognitive habits and technical tools needed to navigate an AI-saturated media environment through five concrete goals:

1. **Bridge the Confidence-Ability Gap:** Give 8-to-17-year-olds practical methods to evaluate AI-generated content and social media rumors instead of relying on gut feeling.
2. **Make Verification Accessible by Voice:** Support voice-in, voice-out verification in Urdu, Indonesian, Spanish, and English across platforms youth already use, including Discord and the web.
3. **Teach Through Socratic Dialogue:** Use guided questions and source-backed explanations so users learn how to spot deceptive framing on their own (UNESCO, 2023).
4. **Equip Youth as Community Guardians:** Help young people verify claims for their families and act as trusted sources of information in their neighborhoods.
5. **Build Local Partnerships:** Work with schools, public libraries, and community health centers through our Trust Nodes network to keep the tool free, grounded, and aligned with SDGs 4, 16, and 17.

---

## 4. Defined Target Audience

* **Primary Users (Ages 8 to 17):** Daily consumers of short-form video feeds and AI tools who need practical methods to detect synthetic media and commercial bias (Ofcom, 2026; APA, 2026). This group is distinct from our team members (aged 18 to 30).
* **Oral-First and Underserved Communities:** Families and workers in areas with good phone coverage but limited written resources, where voice notes in local languages are the primary way news travels.
* **Trust Node Partners:** Teachers, librarians, and community health organizers who run local hubs and help resolve complex cultural claims that automated tools cannot fully evaluate (UNESCO, 2023).

## 5. System Architecture: The Trust Nodes Engine

VeriVoice operates as a hybrid AI-driven verification engine accessible on the Web and through a 24/7 Discord Community Bot ([Live Prototype](https://verivoice-ten.vercel.app) · [Codebase](https://github.com/HamzaKhanBUIC/Veri-Voice-Unesco-Hackathon)):

1. **Multi-Modal Voice & Text Intake:** Users record a voice note via the Web Acoustic Core, upload an audio clip, or type slash commands (`/verify`, `/general`, `/health`, `/climate`, `/disaster`, `/science`) on Discord.
2. **Multilingual Speech Transcription:** Groq Whisper Large v3 (`whisper-large-v3-turbo`) with Speechmatics failover transcribes regional accents and non-Latin scripts (Urdu Nastaliq, Indonesian, Spanish, and English) with measured latency under 400 ms.
3. **15-Domain Intent & Topic Routing:** The request routes deterministically to specialized domain handlers in `DomainDetector.js` covering Health, Earth/Space, Weather/Climate, Geology, Disaster Alerts, Technology, Economics, Law/Policy, Science, Education, Media Literacy, AI Disinformation, Biodiversity, History, and General inquiries.
4. **Hierarchical Source Retrieval:** `SourceAuthorityFilter.js` queries authoritative databases in order:
   * *Tier 1 (Primary Institutional & Scientific Data):* WHO, WMO, UNESCO, UNICEF, PAHO, NOAA, NASA, USGS, Copernicus EU, IPCC.
   * *Tier 2 (Official Regional Government):* NIH/PMD/NDMA/PTA (Pakistan), Kemenkes/BMKG/BNPB/Kominfo (Indonesia), CDC/FDA/FEMA (US), NHS/MetOffice (UK).
   * *Tier 3 (Scientific Review & Research Observatories):* Climate Feedback, Health Feedback, EDMO EU, EU DisinfoLab.
   * *Tier 4 (Certified Fact-Checking Networks):* AFP Fact Check, Reuters, AP News, Snopes, CekFakta, Maldita, Chequeado.
5. **Bounded Groq LPU Reasoning:** Llama 3.3 70B analyzes retrieved text inside strict XML prompt boundaries (`<USER_CLAIM>` and `<EVIDENCE>`) locked at temperature 0.1. The model relies solely on provided sources. If evidence is missing or inconclusive, it returns an explicit `UNCERTAIN` verdict instead of guessing.
6. **Citation Allow-List Validation:** `CitationValidator.js` verifies every source URL against the retrieved institutional set, stripping out fabricated links and blocking dangerous URI schemes (`javascript:`, `data:`, `file:`).
7. **3-Tier Resilient Neural Voice Output:** Microsoft Edge Neural TTS (`ur-PK-UzmaNeural`, `id-ID-GadisNeural`, `es-ES-ElviraNeural`) and ElevenLabs Multilingual v2 (`EXAVITQu4vr4xnSDxMaL` with a 5-key API rotation pool) synthesize natural spoken explanations. Temporary audio files are unlinked immediately after delivery.

## 6. Educational Approach & Key Innovations

1. **Socratic Explanations over Flat Labels:** Instead of simply marking an audio note as false, VeriVoice explains the context conversationally:  
   *“This message claims the local flood barrier broke. However, official NDMA water sensors show normal river levels today. Notice that the forwarded audio did not include a date, location, or speaker name. Those are common signs of unverified emergency rumors.”*  
   This approach builds critical thinking without feeling like a classroom lecture (Muhammad Rafique et al., 2026).
2. **Voice Access for Non-English and Oral Dialects:** By processing spoken Urdu, Indonesian, Spanish, and English directly, VeriVoice allows users who cannot read English fact-checking sites to verify rumors in seconds.
3. **Auditable, Open-Source Pipeline:** All prompt boundaries, authority rankings, and retrieval logic are public on GitHub. Anyone can inspect how claims are checked.

## 7. Scalability & Physical Trust Nodes

1. **Community-Based Scaling:** We partner with local schools, public libraries, and pharmacies to establish physical Trust Nodes.
2. **Quick Start QR Placards:** Visitors scan a QR code on a poster or counter card to start an instant voice check on the web or mobile browser without downloading apps or creating accounts.
3. **Human-in-the-Loop Feedback & Dispute Drawer:** When a claim involves localized slang or disputed neighborhood events, users can submit inquiries through the integrated correction drawer (`[ 🚩 Report / Suggest Correction ]`) for review by trained youth coordinators (Muhammad Rafique et al., 2026).

## 9. Sustainability & UN Sustainable Development Goals

Using fast Groq LPU inference for routine requests combined with client-side idempotency caching keeps running costs under **$0.0012 per complete spoken query**, making the platform affordable for high community volumes.

VeriVoice supports three United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:
* **SDG 4 (Quality Education):** Expands access to media and AI literacy through conversational voice lessons that teach practical verification (UNESCO, 2021; UNESCO, 2023).
* **SDG 16 (Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions):** Helps communities counter unverified rumors, panic, and hate speech before they spread (UNESCO, 2023; United Nations, 2015).
* **SDG 17 (Partnerships for the Goals):** Connects public libraries, youth groups, local clinics, and fact-checking organizations into a shared verification network (United Nations, 2015).
