# UNESCO & MIL Source Authority Audit
**VeriVoice Evidence Authority & Taxonomy Analysis**

---

## 1. Overview & Objective
This audit establishes an empirical, epistemically sound classification for candidate information repositories evaluated by **VeriVoice** in alignment with the **UNESCO Global Youth Hackathon 2026** Media and Information Literacy (MIL) framework.

### Fundamental Principle
$$\text{AUTHORITY} \neq \text{TRUTH}$$
A source possessing high institutional authority does not automatically prove a user's claim. The verification engine independently evaluates:
1. Direct topical relevance to the specific assertion
2. Methodological directness & empirical evidence quality
3. Source context & publication date
4. Source independence (eliminating circular syndicated reporting)
5. Scientific consensus & potential conflicting claims

---

## 2. Granular Authority Taxonomy

| Taxonomy Tier | Definition | Epistemic Weight | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`PRIMARY_INSTITUTIONAL`** | Intergovernmental & global charter bodies establishing international consensus & standards. | Very High | WHO, WMO, UNESCO, PAHO, UNICEF |
| **`PRIMARY_SCIENTIFIC_DATA`** | Primary scientific observational, satellite telemetry, and raw empirical research bodies. | Very High | NASA, NOAA, USGS, ESA |
| **`OFFICIAL_GOVERNMENT`** | Sovereign national ministries, disaster authorities, and public health departments. | High (Regional Scope) | CDC, NDMA (Pakistan), Kemenkes RI (Indonesia), NIH |
| **`SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW`** | Accredited panels of scientists evaluating and peer-reviewing public claims and media reporting. | High (Contextual Review) | Climate Feedback, Science Feedback, Cochrane Reviews |
| **`FACT_CHECKING_ORGANIZATION`** | IFCN-certified professional investigative journalism networks verifying circulating rumors. | Medium-High (Investigative) | AFP Fact Check, Reuters Fact Check, AP Fact Check |
| **`RESEARCH_NETWORK`** | Multi-institution observatories researching cross-border disinformation, deepfakes, and media bias. | Medium-High (Media Research) | EDMO (European Digital Media Observatory) |
| **`SECONDARY_REPUTABLE`** | Peer-reviewed academic journals, major universities, and vetted encyclopedic registries. | Medium-High (Reference) | Nature, The Lancet, Science, Oxford University |
| **`CITIZEN_SCIENCE`** | Open crowd-sourced observational biodiversity and species data networks. | Contextual (Ecological Only) | iNaturalist |
| **`GENERAL_WEB`** | Open public web pages, general portals, and unvetted digital publishers. | Low | General blogs, forums, unverified websites |
| **`UNKNOWN`** | Unrecognized, unreachable, or unresolvable web origins. | Baseline (Zero Weight) | Unclassified domains |

## 3. Individual Organization Audits

### 1. WHO (World Health Organization) — `who.int`, `paho.org`
* **Assigned Tier**: `PRIMARY_INSTITUTIONAL`
* **Primary Domains**: `HEALTH`, `SCIENCE`
* **Evidence Type**: `POLICY_GUIDANCE`, `FACTUAL_EVIDENCE`
* **Reasoning**: UN specialized agency for global health; sets empirical disease guidelines, vaccine protocols, and outbreak mitigation standards.

### 2. CDC (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention) — `cdc.gov`
* **Assigned Tier**: `OFFICIAL_GOVERNMENT`
* **Primary Domains**: `HEALTH`, `DISASTER`
* **Evidence Type**: `FACTUAL_EVIDENCE`, `POLICY_GUIDANCE`
* **Reasoning**: Leading national public health agency; provides clinical surveillance data, immunization safety monitoring, and toxicological guidelines.

### 3. NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — `noaa.gov`, `climate.gov`
* **Assigned Tier**: `PRIMARY_SCIENTIFIC_DATA`
* **Primary Domains**: `WEATHER_CLIMATE`, `EARTH_SPACE`, `DISASTER`
* **Evidence Type**: `FACTUAL_EVIDENCE`, `RESEARCH`
* **Reasoning**: Global scientific authority on oceanic, atmospheric, and paleoclimate data records; provides raw physical sensor measurements and atmospheric models.

### 4. WMO (World Meteorological Organization) — `wmo.int`
* **Assigned Tier**: `PRIMARY_INSTITUTIONAL`
* **Primary Domains**: `WEATHER_CLIMATE`, `DISASTER`
* **Evidence Type**: `POLICY_GUIDANCE`, `FACTUAL_EVIDENCE`
* **Reasoning**: UN specialized agency coordinating standardized meteorological data across 193 member states.

### 5. NASA (National Aeronautics and Space Administration) — `nasa.gov`, `climate.nasa.gov`
* **Assigned Tier**: `PRIMARY_SCIENTIFIC_DATA`
* **Primary Domains**: `EARTH_SPACE`, `SCIENCE`, `WEATHER_CLIMATE`
* **Evidence Type**: `FACTUAL_EVIDENCE`, `RESEARCH`
* **Reasoning**: Premier space and Earth science agency; maintains satellite observation missions measuring atmospheric CO₂, ice sheet mass, and planetary dynamics.

### 6. Climate Feedback (Science Feedback) — `climatefeedback.org`
* **Assigned Tier**: `SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW`
* **Primary Domains**: `WEATHER_CLIMATE`, `SCIENCE`
* **Evidence Type**: `FACT_CHECK`, `RESEARCH`
* **Reasoning**: Accredited worldwide network of PhD scientists assessing the scientific credibility of media claims. Does not replace original raw observation data, but provides scientist-reviewed contextual analysis.

### 7. EDMO (European Digital Media Observatory) — `edmo.eu`
* **Assigned Tier**: `RESEARCH_NETWORK`
* **Primary Domains**: `AI_DISINFORMATION`, `MEDIA_INFORMATION_LITERACY`
* **Evidence Type**: `RESEARCH`, `MEDIA_LITERACY`
* **Reasoning**: Cross-border academic and fact-checking observatory analyzing disinformation campaigns, generative AI synthetic media, and cognitive digital literacy.

### 8. AFP Fact Check — `factcheck.afp.com`, `afp.com`
* **Assigned Tier**: `FACT_CHECKING_ORGANIZATION`
* **Primary Domains**: `GENERAL`, `HEALTH`, `AI_DISINFORMATION`, `SCIENCE`
* **Evidence Type**: `FACT_CHECK`
* **Reasoning**: IFCN-signatory investigative journalistic network verifying circulating viral media across 20+ languages. Highly effective for investigating viral hoaxes, though not the primary scientific originator of empirical datasets.

### 9. iNaturalist — `inaturalist.org`
* **Assigned Tier**: `CITIZEN_SCIENCE`
* **Primary Domains**: `BIODIVERSITY`, `SCIENCE`
* **Evidence Type**: `RESEARCH`, `BACKGROUND`
* **Reasoning**: Joint initiative of California Academy of Sciences and National Geographic providing crowdsourced species occurrence records. Valuable for biodiversity verification, but does not outrank institutional peer-reviewed biological literature.

### 10. NDMA (National Disaster Management Authority) — `ndma.gov.pk`
* **Assigned Tier**: `OFFICIAL_GOVERNMENT`
* **Primary Domains**: `DISASTER`, `WEATHER_CLIMATE`
* **Evidence Type**: `POLICY_GUIDANCE`, `FACTUAL_EVIDENCE`
* **Reasoning**: Pakistan's apex disaster management body; issues official evacuation directives, flood bulletins, and relief advisories.

### 11. Kemenkes RI (Ministry of Health Indonesia) — `kemkes.go.id`
* **Assigned Tier**: `OFFICIAL_GOVERNMENT`
* **Primary Domains**: `HEALTH`
* **Evidence Type**: `POLICY_GUIDANCE`, `FACTUAL_EVIDENCE`
* **Reasoning**: Official executive health ministry of Indonesia; issues localized disease advisories, vaccination directives, and clarifies regional hoaxes.

### 12. UNESCO — `unesco.org`
* **Assigned Tier**: `PRIMARY_INSTITUTIONAL`
* **Primary Domains**: `MEDIA_INFORMATION_LITERACY`, `EDUCATION`, `SCIENCE`
* **Evidence Type**: `POLICY_GUIDANCE`, `MEDIA_LITERACY`
* **Reasoning**: UN agency setting global frameworks for Media and Information Literacy (MIL), cultural heritage preservation, and open science recommendations.
